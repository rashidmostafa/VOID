What accent actually changes

Black stays the primary action colour in both options — accent never takes over the interface. It marks four things and nothing else: the sale price, the sale badge, the rating fill, and the one primary commerce CTA per view. Selected states (size chips, active tab, step marker) move from black to accent so the current choice reads at a glance.

The trade

Greyscale is the stricter read of the tactical direction: labelling discipline and alignment carry the structure, and every colour on screen belongs to the garment. Discounts rely on the struck-through original and the outlined SALE badge, which is quieter than most commerce expects.

Accent buys a legible commerce layer — price drops, stock urgency and the buy action separate from the neutral chrome without a second hue entering the system. The risk is that orange reads closer to conventional retail, and on a page of warm-neutral fashion photography it competes more than black does.
