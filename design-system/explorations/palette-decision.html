What B changes, and only this

  • --fg-link · links carry the accent, keep the underline
  • --commerce-price-sale · discounted price only; standard price stays neutral
  • --commerce-badge-sale-* · SALE becomes a fill; NEW stays black
  • --commerce-rating · rating bar fill
  • --indicator-active · selected size, active tab rule
  • --action-accent-* · one fill per view, on the primary commerce action

Black stays the action colour everywhere else, feedback tones are untouched, and no chart hue moves. Accent coverage is roughly 2% of pixels by design.

The case for each

  • A — merchandise is the only colour on screen, which is the one aesthetic principle your spec keeps (UI-INV-12). Sale, rating and selection read by position, weight and strike-through instead of hue. Costs nothing in accessibility and never fights a photograph.
  • B — signal orange is the marking colour of technical equipment, not of uniforms: hi-vis, instrument dials, tool marking. It gives the data layer a second channel, so a discount or a selected size is legible before you read it. Risk: on a warm-toned garment photo it competes, so it is kept off imagery entirely.
  • Accent values: text oklch(0.52 0.19 45) at 5.94:1 on white; fill oklch(0.66 0.20 50) at 3.33:1 against the page carrying near-black type at 5.39:1. Both clear the gate.
  • If you want the direction but not the hue, the swap is one file: blueprint blue oklch(0.48 0.15 250) reads as engineering drawing rather than equipment.
