Use `Badge` to report state you cannot act on. Anything clickable is a `Tag` or a `Button`.

```jsx
<Badge tone="success" dot>Paid</Badge>
<Badge tone="warning">Awaiting review</Badge>
<Badge tone="solid" size="sm">New in</Badge>
```

Tone map used across the portals: neutral = draft//informational, success = paid or approved, warning = pending or needs attention, danger = failed or rejected, solid = editorial flags on product imagery.
